01 / About Me

Where Textile Engineering Meets Visual Storytelling.

I am Emon Ahmed Mamun, a multidisciplinary Graphics & Apparel Designer with an academic and technical foundation in Textile Engineering. My work bridges creative garment graphics—such as T-shirt color screen prints, export babywear artwork, and sportswear jerseys—with high-impact corporate branding and promotional campaigns.

Alongside my commercial design practice for prominent brands and hospitality groups (including Fortis Group / Fortis Downtown Resort, TORR ramp shows, and international apparel buyers in Thailand), I serve as the Lead Trainer of Computer Operation & Graphics & Design and Training Coordinator at Asia Skills Institution in Rupganj, Narayanganj.

Whether I am preparing color-separated production files for screen printing, designing corporate stationery, or mentoring students in competency-based graphic design programs, my motto remains simple: “Design & Fashion is My Passion.”
